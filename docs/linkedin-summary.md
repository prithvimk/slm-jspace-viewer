# LinkedIn-ready project summary

I built a public Colab mini-course that makes modern representation probing
hands-on: learners move from visible reasoning traces to residual-stream
analysis, compare logit and Jacobian lenses, and interact with compact J-space
artifacts on a free Colab T4. The course is explicit about an important limit:
interpretability probes provide evidence for hypotheses about representations;
they are not a transcript of a model's hidden private reasoning.
